## Overview

DARWIN uses the exact visual language of the deployed app: a dark operating
system for evidence-driven distribution. It is dense, calm and precise rather
than futuristic or decorative.

## The Frame

Every scene sits on the app's 64 px technical grid. Use near-black surfaces,
one-pixel dividers, console-like zones and monospace status labels. Inter carries
the claims; JetBrains Mono carries steps, sources, states and coordinates.

## Composition Rules

- Use rectangular panels, thin rules and asymmetric zone-based layouts.
- Use the official transparent DARWIN wordmark without stretching or redrawing it.
- Green means active, sourced, connected or selected.
- Orange means rejected or failing; amber means a warning or friction.
- White is reserved for the main claim and decisive CTA surfaces.
- Scale web UI patterns up for a 1920×1080 stage.

## Motion Character

Motion behaves like the product: a status comes online, a trace travels, evidence
locks to a promise, weak candidates recede and the winning path returns to memory.
Use short controlled entrances and one continuous live signal per scene.

## Do Not

- Do not use the previous paper/coral editorial palette.
- Do not use serif type, orbs, glass, gradients in text or rounded dashboard cards.
- Do not invent or redraw the logo.
- Do not mix the landing green with unrelated accent colors.
