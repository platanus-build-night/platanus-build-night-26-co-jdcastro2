## Overview

DARWIN looks like an editorial field report from a living system: restrained,
precise and warm, with coral used as biological signal rather than decoration.

## The Frame

Alternate ink and paper surfaces. Every frame has at least three layers:
atmospheric grain or ghost typography, the main claim or evidence, and a
foreground system of rules, coordinates or labels.

## Composition Rules

- Use hard edges, hairline rules and asymmetric layouts.
- Treat type as the primary image; charts and flows are structural, never dashboard UI.
- Anchor focal content to an edge and give the eye a second destination.
- Use one coral signal per frame; do not distribute accent color evenly.
- Large words may bleed outside the frame when they are decorative.

## Motion Character

Motion behaves like selection and evolution: fragments assemble, evidence
travels, nodes activate, weak signals recede and one path locks in. Entrances
are confident and decelerating; the system slide is the motion peak; the close
returns to stillness.

## Do Not

- Do not use gradients inside headline text.
- Do not use rounded dashboard cards, glossy glass or neon technology motifs.
- Do not use the rejected branching-D logo.
- Do not turn every element into an animation; stillness is part of the pacing.
